The viaduct network

viaduct.sh operates tunnel nodes in five regions. Run your tunnel on the one nearest your traffic; a tunnel always terminates at your machine, so pick the node closest to you and your visitors.

RegionEndpointStatus
London defaultviaduct.shchecking
New Yorknyc.viaduct.shchecking
Singaporesg.viaduct.shchecking
Sydneysyd.viaduct.shchecking
Bangaloreblr.viaduct.shchecking

Pick a region with viaduct http 8080 --region nyc, or run viaduct regions to list them. Full details in the regions docs.
