The viaduct network

viaduct.sh operates tunnel nodes in four regions. Run your tunnel on the one nearest your traffic; a tunnel always terminates at your machine, so pick the node closest to you and your visitors.

RegionEndpoint
London defaultviaduct.sh:4443
New Yorknyc.viaduct.sh:4443
Singaporesg.viaduct.sh:4443
Sydneysyd.viaduct.sh:4443

Pick a region with viaduct http 8080 --region nyc, or run viaduct regions to list them. Full details in the regions docs.
